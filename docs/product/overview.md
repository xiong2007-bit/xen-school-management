# Product overview

XEN SCHOOL MANAGEMENT v3.0 is a commercial multi-tenant SaaS platform for primary, secondary and combined schools. Zimbabwe is the launch market, followed by international expansion.

## Four connected experiences
1. Individually designed public school websites.
2. Private school management workspace.
3. Parent/guardian portal and optional student portal.
4. XEN platform administration console.

The product includes school onboarding, people, academics, attendance, assessments, timetables, admissions, school finance, communication, portals, website builder/CMS, domains, library, transport, documents, service desk, reporting and XEN SaaS subscriptions. Folder presence is not evidence of implementation.
